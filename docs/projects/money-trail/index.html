THE ENGINEERING QUESTION

A financial result should come with a trail of evidence.

An outstanding invoice is easy to display and harder to justify. Money Trail addresses the engineering behind trustworthy financial control: which payments count, which dates apply, whether the sources are complete and how a reviewer can trace a result back to the original record. It connects the concerns of ERP integration with analytics engineering and controlled publication.

Extract once, preserve what was received

Python accepts manifests with declared coverage, validates file hashes and imports source records into PostgreSQL. The importer fingerprints bundles and preserves source evidence. Odoo extraction and conversion are separate modules, so ERP payloads are transformed into the same explicit reconciliation contract rather than being treated as already trustworthy analytical facts. Company, currency and import batch are part of the scope.

Model invoice reconciliation precisely

dbt staging normalizes source data; core models calculate the component amounts. An invoice balance subtracts eligible confirmed payment allocations and posted credit notes from the invoice amount. Source states and the control date constrain eligibility. A payment context record supports the evidence but is not counted again as a second financial contribution. Source coverage is carried through the calculation: missing information must not silently become a known zero.

Bank settlement is a different claim

The settlement model compares captured payments minus refunds and fees, plus signed adjustments, with eligible bank-originated receipts and confirmed allocations. The local Odoo invoice/payment import does not establish that money reached a bank account. Candidate bank matches remain suggestions. Approving a human proposal does not update Odoo. This boundary prevents a plausible dashboard from implying a stronger accounting conclusion than its sources support.

Publish a validated version, not tables halfway through a build

Each publication uses an isolated candidate schema prefix and a declared set of source snapshots. The publication code verifies matching dbt invocation identities, all expected model results and business tests, destination schemas and evidence integrity. Advisory locking serializes publication. Validated results and dossiers are archived; a failed or interrupted later candidate does not replace the previous usable version. Source snapshots are versions, not additional business activity to sum together.

Implementation & evidencepublication.py

Give review its own data model

The interface brings a result, its component records and a documented investigation into one workflow. Cases support assignment, comments, proposals and independent approval or rejection. Server-side roles and expected revisions constrain decisions and stale updates. Administrative closure, accepting a justified gap and resolution after a new control have different meanings. File and row hashes support traceability; they do not certify that the original external record was true.

What the available validation establishes

The current validation guide records an owner-reported Windows run on 6 October 2026: 89 Odoo records imported, 18 dbt models and 21 data tests completed, 18 invoice results and zero settlement results published. It separately records 21 mocked-service Streamlit AppTest cases. Private raw exports and full operational logs are excluded from the public repository; these integration observations are reported evidence, not a publicly reproducible throughput measurement or accounting certification.

Scope & limitations

  • Odoo invoice reconciliation is not bank settlement confirmation.
  • The local integration counts are owner-reported; private operational logs are not public.
  • The portfolio project does not claim accounting certification, bank integration acceptance or cloud deployment.
  • Human approval does not write back to Odoo.
