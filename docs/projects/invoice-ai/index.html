THE ENGINEERING QUESTION

OCR is a draft. Review turns it into a usable record.

Extracted invoice text is not automatically reliable accounting data. This prototype makes recognition, parsing, arithmetic and human approval separate steps, so an apparently plausible total cannot bypass review.

A visible review boundary

Pretrained PaddleOCR models recognize the image. Explicit extraction rules build headers, parties and items; the English parser reconstructs coordinate-based multiline tables. Decimal checks compare rounded line totals and subtotal plus tax minus discount. Streamlit lets the reviewer compare the image and fields, correct them and save an approval record with source hash and history. Editing invalidates prior confirmation.

Test the covered layouts honestly

The repository records 13 regression/replay tests across known French samples, English OCR boxes, missing cells and ambiguous dates. These fixtures are not a representative accuracy benchmark. An unfamiliar quote produced empty fields/items in an initial check. No LLM is used, and an arithmetic PASS establishes neither OCR accuracy nor document authenticity.

Scope & limitations

  • Layout-specific extraction; unfamiliar invoices may be incomplete.
  • No general multipage PDF, complex tax, credit-note or quote classification.
  • Local reviewer identity is not authenticated; MAD/EUR only.
